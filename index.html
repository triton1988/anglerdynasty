Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems: write to the developer through the contact on the game's App Store page. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

  • Lost a purchase after reinstalling? Open Menu → Settings → Restore.
  • Change the language: Menu → Settings → Language (9 languages).
  • The game works offline and has no accounts.
